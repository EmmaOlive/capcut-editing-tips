# CapCut Editing Tips and Tricks

Welcome to CapCut Editing Tips, a beginner-friendly resource for learning video editing techniques and improving creative projects.

## What This Repository Covers

- Basic video editing techniques
- Smooth transitions and visual effects
- Adding captions and text overlays
- Background music and audio adjustments
- Editing short-form videos for social media
- Exporting videos with suitable settings

## Explore CapCut

If you want to explore desktop video editing features, visit the official [CapCut Desktop Video Editor](https://www.capcut.com/tools/desktop-video-editor).

## Tips for Better Videos

1. Plan your clips before editing.
2. Keep transitions consistent.
3. Make text easy to read.
4. Balance music and voice audio.
5. Preview your video before exporting.
